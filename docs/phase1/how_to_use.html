Step 1: Onboarding & YouTube Connection

First Time Setup

Connect Your YouTube Channel

When Priya first opens Creator OS, she sees the landing page with a prominent "Connect YouTube" button.

What Priya Does:

  1. Click "Connect with YouTube" on the landing page
  2. Google's OAuth consent screen appears — Priya reviews the permissions (read channel data, analytics, captions)
  3. Click "Allow" to grant access
  4. She is automatically redirected back to the Creator OS dashboard

What Happens Behind the Scenes:

  • Creator OS receives an OAuth token and securely encrypts it in the database
  • The platform fetches Priya's channel info: name, subscriber count, video list
  • Her account is created and she's logged in
Tip: Creator OS only requests read-only access to your YouTube data. It never uploads, deletes, or modifies anything on your channel.

Step 2: Build Your Creator DNA

Important - Do This First

Teach the AI Your Voice

Before using any content generation features, Priya needs to build her Creator DNA fingerprint. This is what makes Creator OS output sound like her, not generic AI.

What Priya Does:

  1. Navigate to Profile in the sidebar
  2. Click "Build Creator DNA"
  3. Wait 2–5 minutes while the AI analyzes her videos
  4. Review the DNA profile that appears: a radar chart and voice characteristics

What Happens Behind the Scenes:

  • Creator OS fetches transcripts from Priya's 20 most recent videos
  • Claude AI analyzes each transcript for 14 stylistic dimensions (vocabulary, humor, pacing, formality, etc.)
  • Results are aggregated into a single Creator DNA Fingerprint
  • A 768-dimensional embedding vector is generated and stored
  • Confidence score is calculated (with 20 videos: ~90%)

Step 4: Generate Content Ideas

Creative Phase

Turn Trends into Video Ideas

Priya picks the Cursor AI trend and wants to brainstorm specific video ideas around it.

What Priya Does:

  1. Navigate to Ideas in the sidebar (or click "Generate Ideas" on a trend card)
  2. Click "Generate Ideas"
  3. Optionally provide context: "I want ideas about AI coding tools, my audience loves practical tutorials"
  4. Wait 30–60 seconds while the AI brainstorms
  5. Review 8–12 idea cards, each with:
    • Title and hook
    • Viability score (1–10)
    • Content type (tutorial, review, listicle, etc.)
    • Key talking points
    • Difficulty estimate
  6. Review each idea — Approve the ones she likes, Reject the rest

Production Phase

Priya approves the Cursor AI idea and wants a full video script.

What Priya Does:

  2. Wait 1–2 minutes while the script is generated

  4. The result appears in the Script Editor with collapsible sections:
    • Cold Open / Hook (first 30 seconds)

    • Climax / Key Insight
    • Call to Action
    • Outro
  5. Review the script and make any edits directly in the editor
  6. Check the variant tabs for auto-generated versions:
    • YouTube Shorts script (60-second version)
    • Blog post version
    • Twitter/X thread version
    • LinkedIn article version
    • Newsletter version
  7. Approve the script to move it to the next stage

Step 8: Analyze Video Performance

After Publishing

Learn What Works and Improve

After Priya publishes her video, Creator OS automatically tracks its performance.

What Priya Does:

  1. Navigate to Analytics in the sidebar
  2. See an overview dashboard with:
    • Channel metrics: total views, subscribers gained, watch hours
    • Video-level performance: views, CTR, avg. view duration, retention curve
    • AI Insights: automatically generated analysis explaining why a video performed well or poorly
  3. Read insights like: "Your Cursor AI video has 2.3x higher CTR than your channel average. The question-format title and split-screen thumbnail drove higher click rates."

What Happens Behind the Scenes:

  • The Performance Analyst Agent syncs YouTube analytics every 6 hours (automatic via Celery)
  • It computes derived metrics (engagement rate, growth velocity, audience retention patterns)
  • Detects patterns across videos (best publishing times, optimal video length, top-performing formats)
  • Generates actionable insights using AI analysis
  • Crucially: These insights are fed back into the other agents:
    • Trend Scout learns which topic areas perform best for Priya
    • Idea Generator adjusts idea scoring based on what actually works
    • Script Architect learns preferred video length and pacing
    • SEO Optimizer learns which title patterns drive the highest CTR
Tip: The feedback loop means Creator OS gets smarter the more you use it. After 10+ videos tracked, the recommendations become significantly more personalized.

Pro Tips for Getting the Most Out of Creator OS

1. Build DNA First

Always build your Creator DNA before generating scripts. Without it, the AI writes generic content instead of matching your voice.

2. Edit, Don't Rewrite

The AI gets you 80% there. Spend your time adding personal anecdotes and polishing the last 20% rather than rewriting from scratch.

3. Use the Full Pipeline

Running Trend → Idea → Script → SEO as a single pipeline saves time and ensures each stage builds on the previous one.

4. Check Insights Weekly

The Performance Analyst gets smarter with more data. Review its insights weekly to understand what's working and why.

5. Use All Variants

Don't just use the YouTube script. Post the blog version, share the Twitter thread, publish the newsletter. Multiply your content's reach.

6. Refresh DNA After Pivots

If you change your content style or niche, manually trigger a DNA rebuild from the Profile page to update the AI's understanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Creator OS upload videos to YouTube?

No. In Phase 1, Creator OS is read-only for YouTube. It fetches your analytics and channel data but never uploads, modifies, or deletes anything on your channel. You copy the script, title, description, and tags manually to YouTube Studio.

Is my YouTube data safe?

Yes. Your YouTube API tokens are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption. Creator OS only requests read-only scopes. Your data never leaves your self-hosted instance.

How much does the AI usage cost?

Creator OS uses the Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI APIs, which are billed by token usage. A typical full pipeline run (trend + idea + script + SEO) costs approximately $0.10–$0.30 in API credits depending on script length.

How many videos does it need for good DNA?

The DNA system produces useful results from as few as 5 videos (55% confidence). At 20 videos it reaches 90% confidence. For best results, have at least 10 published videos.

Can I use it for non-YouTube platforms?

The script variants (blog, Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletter) can be used for any platform. However, the analytics and SEO features are YouTube-specific in Phase 1.

What happens if I don't like the script?

Every human gate lets you approve, reject, or edit. If you reject, you can regenerate with different parameters or provide specific feedback. The AI will try a different approach.
